Forum PolicyVault

A non-custodial spend policy for an AI agent, live on Arc. Every number on this page is read from the chain in your browser.

Unaudited, capped pilot. The contract holds no funds — it moves USDC directly from the owner's wallet under an allowance, so approve(0) revokes it instantly. The allowance is the real cap. The in-contract budgets are bucketed (UTC-midnight days, 30-day epochs anchored at deploy), so twice a cap is reachable across a boundary by design.
